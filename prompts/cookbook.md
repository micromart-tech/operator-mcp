# Example prompts

Questions that work well with the Micromart connector. Adjust names, dates, and
locations to your operation.

## Sales & trends

- "How were sales last week compared to the week before?"
- "Show me revenue by day for the last 30 days."
- "What are my peak selling hours?"
- "How did this month compare to last month?"

## Stores & cabinets

- "Which of my stores are underperforming this month?"
- "Rank my stores by revenue for October."
- "Which cabinets at the airport location sell the most?"
- "Give me an overview of my business."

## Products

- "What are my top 10 products by revenue?"
- "How is [product name] selling?"
- "Which products are frequently bought together?"
- "What's my worst-selling product this quarter?"

## Planograms & merchandising

- "What does the planogram look like for cabinet [name]?"
- "Are there pending planogram changes for cabinet M-1234?"
- "Which shelf row sells best at the office location?"
- "Where should I place [new product] in my cabinets?"

## Restocking & inventory

- "Which cabinets need a restock visit first?"
- "What's out of stock right now?"
- "Plan a restock route for my downtown stores."
- "How much [product] is left at [location]?"

## Operations digest

- "Give me this week's operations digest."
- "Anything unusual in my sales data this week?"
